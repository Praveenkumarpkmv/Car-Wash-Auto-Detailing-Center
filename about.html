Our Story

About AquaShine

Passionate about cars, committed to perfection. Discover the people and principles behind the shine.

Since 2013

Driven by Passion, Defined by Quality

AquaShine started in a small garage in Los Angeles with one simple belief: every car deserves to look and feel brand new. What began as a one-man operation has grown into the city's most trusted name in premium car care.

Over a decade later, we've detailed more than 5,000 vehicles — from daily drivers to exotic supercars. Our team combines old-school craftsmanship with cutting-edge ceramic technology, always using eco-friendly products that are safe for your car and the planet.

We're not just washing cars. We're protecting investments, restoring pride, and delivering that unforgettable showroom feeling — every single time.
