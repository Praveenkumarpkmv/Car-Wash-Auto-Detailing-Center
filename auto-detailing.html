What is Detailing?

More Than a Wash — It's Restoration

Auto detailing is an intricate, multi-stage process that goes far beyond a regular car wash. It involves deep cleaning, polishing, and protecting every surface of your vehicle — both inside and out — to restore it to its original showroom condition.

At AquaShine, our certified detailers use professional-grade products, machine polishers, and years of hands-on experience to eliminate swirl marks, remove stubborn stains, and bring out a depth of shine that simply can't be achieved with a standard wash.

Whether you're preparing for a car show, selling your vehicle, or simply want to fall in love with your car again — our detailing services deliver results you can see and feel.

How It Works

Our Detailing Process

A proven 5-step process refined over a decade of detailing thousands of vehicles.

1

Inspection

We assess paint condition, interior wear, and identify areas needing special attention.

2

Decontamination

Clay bar and iron remover eliminate embedded contaminants from the paint surface.

3

Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing removes swirls, scratches, and oxidation.

4

Protection

Premium wax, sealant, or ceramic coating locks in the shine and protects the paint.

5

Final Touch

Interior dressing, tire shine, and a quality inspection under professional lighting.
