The Science

What is a Ceramic Coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer — typically silicon dioxide (SiO₂) or silicon carbide (SiC) based — that chemically bonds with your vehicle's clear coat. Unlike traditional wax or sealant that simply sits on top of the paint, ceramic coating forms a semi-permanent layer that becomes part of the surface itself.

Once cured, the coating creates a highly cross-linked, glass-like shell that is incredibly hard, chemically resistant, and extremely hydrophobic. Water beads and rolls off, dirt and contaminants struggle to bond, and UV rays are deflected before they can fade or oxidize your paint.

The result isn't just a glossier car — it's a car that stays cleaner, looks newer, resists damage, and requires less work to maintain. That's the AquaShine ceramic promise.

Aftercare

Caring for Your Coating

A few simple habits will keep your ceramic coating performing at its best for years.

Wash Regularly
Wash every 2-4 weeks using a pH-neutral soap. Avoid automatic brush washes, which can scratch the coating and dull its gloss.
Two-Bucket Method
Use two buckets — one for soap, one for rinsing. This prevents dirt from being reintroduced to the paint and marring the surface.
Avoid Abrasives
Never use abrasive polishes, compounds, or brushes on a coated car. These will strip the coating and require re-application.
Shade Dry
Dry your car in the shade with a soft microfiber towel. Avoid sun-drying, which can leave water spots that etch the coating.
Use Ceramic Boost
Apply the included ceramic boost spray every 2-3 months to rejuvenate hydrophobics and add an extra sacrificial layer of protection.
Annual Inspection
Bring your car in once a year for a free coating inspection. We'll assess performance and recommend any booster or touch-ups.

Important: Ceramic Coating is Not Scratch-Proof

While a 9H ceramic coating provides excellent resistance against light swirls, chemical etching, and environmental contaminants, it is not a substitute for paint protection film (PPF). It will not prevent rock chips, deep scratches, or impact damage. For maximum protection, we recommend pairing ceramic coating with clear bra on high-impact areas.
