Complete Service Menu

Every Service We Offer

A full breakdown of our professional services, from the simplest wash to the most advanced paint correction.

Express Hand Wash

A meticulous two-bucket hand wash with pH-neutral soap, soft microfiber mitts, and a gentle hand dry. The safest wash your car can get.

20-30 min Hand Wash Eco Soap

Premium Wash & Wax

Everything in the Express Wash, plus a protective hand-applied carnauba wax that adds depth, gloss, and months of paint protection.

45-60 min Carnauba Wax Deep Gloss

Interior Deep Clean

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, leather conditioning, plastic dressing, and complete odor removal for a fresh, like-new cabin.

2-3 hours Steam Clean Odor Removal

Clay Bar Decontamination

Removes embedded industrial fallout, tree sap, and overspray that washing can't touch. Leaves paint silky smooth and ready for protection.

1-2 hours Decontamination Smooth Finish

1-Step Paint Correction

Single-stage machine polish that removes light swirl marks, oxidation, and haze. Delivers noticeable gloss improvement in a single pass.

3-4 hours Machine Polish Gloss Boost

Multi-Stage Paint Correction

Two or three-stage compounding and polishing that removes even deep scratches, holograms, and defects. Concours-level refinement for your paint.

6-10 hours Show Car Finish Mirror Gloss

Ceramic Coating (9H)

A professional-grade 9H ceramic layer that chemically bonds to your paint for years of hydrophobic protection, UV resistance, and incredible gloss.

1-2 days 2-5 Years Hydrophobic

Headlight Restoration

Wet-sanding and polishing of cloudy, yellowed headlights to restore clarity. Finished with a UV-protective sealant for long-lasting results.

1-2 hours UV Seal Better Visibility

Engine Bay Detail

Safe degreasing, gentle rinse, and dressing of all plastic and rubber components. Makes maintenance easier and boosts resale value.

45-60 min Safe Degreaser Plastic Dressing

Odor Elimination

Ozone treatment that neutralizes smoke, pet, mildew, and food odors at the molecular level. Eliminates smells, not just masks them.

1-2 hours Ozone Full Cabin

Leather Care & Conditioning

Gentle pH-balanced cleaning followed by a premium conditioner that restores suppleness, prevents cracking, and adds UV protection.

1-2 hours pH-Balanced UV Protect

Glass & Mirror Treatment

Deep-cleaned glass inside and out, plus a hydrophobic rain-repellent coating on exterior windows that beads water and improves visibility.

30-45 min Rain Repellent Streak-Free

Wheel & Tire Deep Clean

Iron remover, wheel-well cleaning, brake dust removal, and a long-lasting tire dressing that gives your wheels a deep black finish.

30-45 min Iron Remover Tire Shine

Full Concours Detail

Our ultimate package: full interior + exterior + multi-stage paint correction + ceramic coating. Reserved for those who demand absolute perfection.

2-3 days Ultimate Package Show Car
